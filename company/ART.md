# Hani's Blog: art direction

This company's art follows the city's shared style, `system3-city ART.md` (true isometric, light from the upper left,
three tones per face from the city's palette, labels on surfaces, original logos, subtle motion that is safe under
reduced motion). This file keeps what is particular to Hani's Blog. The city's illustration draws it in system3-meta
`hq/city-scene.js` (`hq/ART.md`).

## The metaphor

**A printing café**: people read and talk; the essays are printed in the back. The acorn hangs at the door.

## Its building in the city

- **The building:** a two-storey amber café with a red pitched roof, a striped awning, tables with umbrellas outside, and the acorn on a hanging sign.
- **Live data it shows:** a crane while a milestone is active; people at its door for the members working in the last hour.
- **The roof sign:** its logo, on a light board, lit at night.

## Accent colours

- **Accent:** amber `#e0a24a`; red and paper stripes on the awning.
- **Status colours:** the city's reserved status colours only (running, ended, onboarding, blocked).

## The logo

`data/logo.svg`: a smiling acorn with a fluffy cap getting a haircut from a pair of scissors; the author's own mark (vectorised, 2026-10-26).

## Its HQ pages and products

Its HQ pages use the HQ's own tokens (light and dark, phone width 390) and the city's page templates; nothing on a page imitates the illustration. **Its demos** (the posts' interactive demos and the `/demos` gallery) keep the site's own design, but share the city's rules where they overlap: light and dark, phone width, motion that is safe under reduced motion, and labels that sit on what they name.
